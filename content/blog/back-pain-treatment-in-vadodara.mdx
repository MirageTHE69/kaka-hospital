### Common Causes of Back Pain

Back pain may arise from various underlying conditions. The most common include:

- **Muscle Strain:** Often due to poor posture or sudden movement.
- **Slip Disc / Herniated Disc:** Pressure on nerves causing sharp, radiating pain.
- **Spondylosis:** Age-related degeneration of vertebrae.
- **Sciatica:** Pain radiating from lower back to legs.
- **Spinal Stenosis:** Narrowing of the spinal canal.
- **Arthritis:** Inflammation affecting spinal joints.

In Vadodara, prolonged sitting in office environments and reduced physical exercise contribute largely to chronic back issues.

---

### Symptoms You Should Not Ignore

Dr. Alpesh Parekh recommends looking out for the following red flags:

- Persistent back pain lasting more than a week
- Tingling, numbness, or radiating pain in legs
- Difficulty bending or standing straight
- Sudden muscle weakness
- Pain after an accident or fall

Such symptoms indicate the need for immediate medical evaluation.

### When Should You Visit an Orthopedic Spine Specialist?

You should consult **Dr. Alpesh Parekh** when:

- Home remedies are not working
- Pain affects your sleep or daily routine
- You feel stiffness after waking up
- You experience nerve-related symptoms
- Pain increases during walking, bending, or lifting

Timely diagnosis ensures that the condition does not worsen into chronic pain or mobility issues.

### Effective Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the cause of pain and severity. At KK Multispeciality Hospital, Vadodara, back pain is treated with a combination of:

#### 1. Lifestyle Changes

- Improving sitting posture
- Avoiding heavy lifting
- Regular stretching

#### 2. Medication

- Pain relievers
- Anti-inflammatory medicines
- Muscle relaxants

#### 3. Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy plays a major role in long-term recovery. It helps in:

- Strengthening back muscles
- Improving posture
- Reducing stiffness
- Increasing flexibility

#### 4. Advanced Treatments

For severe or persistent pain:

- Epidural injections
- Nerve root blocks
- Minimally invasive spine surgery (if required)

Dr. Alpesh Parekh believes in starting with the least invasive approach and using surgery only when absolutely necessary.
