## Causes, Early Signs & Solutions by Dr. Alpesh Parekh

Knee pain has become one of the most common orthopedic complaints among people in Vadodara. Whether it’s due to long working hours, lifestyle changes, sports injuries, or age-related wear and tear, knee discomfort often affects daily life more than we realize. According to **Dr. Alpesh Parekh**, a leading orthopedic surgeon in Vadodara, timely diagnosis and early treatment can prevent long-term damage and help patients return to pain-free living.

### Common Causes of Knee Pain

Knee pain can occur due to multiple reasons. The most common include:

- **Osteoarthritis:** Age-related joint wear causing stiffness and swelling.
- **Ligament Injuries:** ACL, MCL tears usually seen in sports injuries.
- **Meniscus Tear:** Occurs when twisting or lifting heavy weight.
- **Patellofemoral Pain Syndrome:** Pain around the kneecap due to muscle imbalance.
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis:** Autoimmune condition leading to chronic inflammation.

In Vadodara, sudden weather changes, sedentary jobs, and unbalanced exercise routines have increased knee-related issues in younger adults too.

### Early Signs You Should Not Ignore

Dr. Alpesh Parekh highlights a few warning signs:

- Morning stiffness lasting more than 20 minutes
- Pain while climbing stairs
- Difficulty sitting cross-legged
- Swelling or warmth around the joint
- Clicking, popping, or feeling of instability

Ignoring these symptoms may worsen the condition and limit movement.

### When Should You Consult an Orthopedic Surgeon?

You should visit an orthopedic specialist in Vadodara when:

- Pain continues for more than a week
- Pain interferes with work, walking, or sleep
- You’ve had a fall or injury
- You experience sudden swelling

Dr. Alpesh Parekh strongly recommends early assessment to avoid long-term joint damage.

### Effective Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the underlying cause:

1. **Lifestyle Modifications:**

Weight control
2. Low-impact exercises like walking, cycling, swimming
3. **Medications:**

Anti-inflammatory medicines
4. Supplements like vitamin D or calcium
5. **Physiotherapy:**

Strengthening thigh and core muscles
6. Improving knee alignment
7. **Advanced Treatments:**

PRP injections
8. Viscosupplementation
9. Arthroscopic surgery (minimally invasive)
10. **Knee Replacement Surgery:** Recommended when joint damage is severe and other methods fail.
