## Common Causes of Shoulder Pain

Shoulder pain can occur due to multiple reasons. The most common include:

### 1. Rotator Cuff Injury

The rotator cuff helps shoulder movement. Overuse or sudden strain causes:

- Sharp pain
- Weakness
- Difficulty lifting the arm

### 2. Frozen Shoulder (Adhesive Capsulitis)

This condition causes stiffness and restricted movement. It develops more often in people with:

- Diabetes
- Thyroid disorders
- Prolonged immobilization

### 3. Tendonitis

Inflammation of shoulder tendons due to repetitive use — common in swimmers, cleaners, gym-goers.

### 4. Shoulder Arthritis

Age-related wear causing grinding, stiffness, and pain during movement.

### 5. Bursitis

Inflammation of the shoulder bursa causing swelling and discomfort.

## Symptoms You Should Not Ignore

Dr. Alpesh Parekh highlights these warning signs:

- Difficulty raising your arm
- Shoulder stiffness in the morning
- Pain while sleeping on the affected side
- Clicking or popping sound
- Weakness while performing normal activities
- Swelling or visible deformity

If these symptoms persist for more than a week, professional evaluation is necessary.

---

## Understanding Frozen Shoulder

Frozen shoulder is a common condition in Vadodara, especially in people aged 40–60. The shoulder gradually becomes:

- Painful
- Stiff
- Difficult to rotate
- Limited in upward movements

It develops in stages:

1. **Freezing Stage:** Increasing pain
2. **Frozen Stage:** Stiffness dominates
3. **Thawing Stage:** Slow improvement

Without treatment, frozen shoulder may take up to **2 years** to fully resolve.

## Treatment Options by Dr. Alpesh Parekh

Treatment depends on the cause of pain. At KK Multispeciality Hospital, Vadodara, the approach includes:

### 1. Medications

- Anti-inflammatory tablets
- Pain relievers
- Short-term steroids (only when required)

### 2. Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy is the backbone of frozen shoulder treatment. It helps in:

- Improving mobility
- Reducing stiffness
- Strengthening shoulder muscles

### 3. Hot & Cold Therapy

- Heat reduces stiffness
- Ice reduces swelling

### 4. Ultrasound Therapy

Used by physiotherapists to reduce inflammation.

### 5. Injections

Corticosteroid injections may be used for severe inflammation.

### 6. Arthroscopic Surgery

Only recommended when pain or stiffness does not improve with non-surgical treatments.

## Home Care Tips for Shoulder Pain

- Avoid heavy lifting
- Do gentle stretching exercises
- Maintain correct posture
- Apply ice or heat therapy
- Avoid sudden jerky movements
