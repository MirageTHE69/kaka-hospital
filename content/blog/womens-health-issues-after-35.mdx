## Expert Solutions by Dr. Ranjana Parekh, Vadodara

Women experience several physical and hormonal changes after the age of 35. These changes are natural, but when ignored, they can lead to long-term health concerns. In Vadodara, many women delay consulting a specialist due to busy schedules, family responsibilities, or lack of awareness. According to **Dr. Ranjana Parekh**, one of the most trusted gynecologists in Vadodara, early screening and timely care help prevent complications and ensure long-term well-being.

This blog highlights the most common women’s health issues after 35 and offers medically accurate, easy-to-follow solutions.

---

## 1. Hormonal Imbalances

Hormonal fluctuations are common for women above 35. These may cause:

- Irregular periods
- Mood changes
- Weight gain
- Fatigue
- Acne or hair fall

**Solution:** Dr. Ranjana Parekh recommends simple blood tests to check estrogen, progesterone, thyroid, and other hormone levels. Treatment may include lifestyle changes, vitamins, and guided hormonal therapy based on individual needs.

---

## 2. Menstrual Irregularities

Women in Vadodara often complain of:

- Delayed periods
- Heavy bleeding
- Very short cycles
- Painful periods

These symptoms may indicate PCOS, thyroid problems, uterine fibroids, or perimenopause.

**Solution:** Dr. Ranjana Parekh advises regular monitoring and ultrasound scans. Proper medication, balanced diet, and stress reduction can significantly improve cycle regularity.

---

## 3. Early Signs of Menopause

Many women start experiencing early menopause symptoms between 35–45 years:

- Hot flashes
- Sleep disturbance
- Weight gain
- Joint pain
- Reduced energy

**Solution:** Early detection is key. Dr. Ranjana Parekh recommends a combination of hormonal evaluation, lifestyle modification, and specific supplements to ease symptoms and maintain overall bone and heart health.

---

## 4. Cervical and Breast Health

After 35, screening becomes extremely important. In Vadodara, many women still skip basic tests, leading to late diagnosis of preventable conditions.

### Essential Tests Dr. Ranjana Parekh Suggests:

- **Pap Smear:** To detect cervical cancer early
- **Cervical Cancer Vaccination**
- **Breast Examination**
- **Ultrasound or Mammography (if required)**

These tests help detect abnormalities early when treatment is most effective.

---

## 5. Pregnancy After 35

Women can have safe pregnancies after 35, but certain risks increase, such as:

- Gestational diabetes
- High blood pressure
- Miscarriage risk
- Low egg reserve

**Solution:** With proper antenatal care from an experienced gynecologist like Dr. Ranjana Parekh, most complications can be avoided. Regular check-ups, diet planning, and fetal monitoring ensure a safe and healthy pregnancy.

---

## 6. Bone and Joint Health

Women above 35 may experience:

- Calcium deficiency
- Vitamin D deficiency
- Early signs of osteoporosis

**Solution:** Calcium-rich diet, sunlight exposure, vitamin D supplements, and weight-bearing exercises help maintain bone strength.
